Questions

Frequently asked

Will callers know it is an AI?

It sounds genuinely human — natural pauses, interruption handling and real emotion in the voice. Many callers do not notice. We still recommend being upfront where your industry or local regulation expects it, and the agent can be told to disclose it.

How long does it take to go live?

Most people have a working agent in under ten minutes and a real test call placed the same afternoon. Buying an Indian number is the only step that can take longer, since it needs KYC.

Do I need a phone number to try it?

No. You can talk to your agent from the browser using the web voice widget, or place a test call to your own mobile. Buy or import a number only when you are ready to go live.

Can it really handle Hindi and English in one call?

Yes. Select both languages and the agent detects what the caller is speaking and switches mid-conversation — including most major Indian languages.

What happens if it does not know an answer?

It says so honestly rather than inventing something, and hands off to a human or takes a callback request — exactly as you instruct it in the training sections.

Is my data kept separate from other clients?

Yes. Every account is isolated, agents and call data are scoped to their owner, and nothing is shared across accounts.

Can I run this for my own clients under my brand?

That is exactly what it is built for. Run multiple client accounts from one platform, set your own per-minute rate for each, and keep your branding throughout.

What do I actually pay for?

Only connected talk time, at ₹12 per minute. Building agents, testing, the knowledge base, analytics and the dashboard cost nothing extra.
